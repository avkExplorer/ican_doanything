What I make

Four things, made by hand

Every product starts as a raw material — plaster, thread, acrylic, or a tiny chip — and ends up as something a kid actually keeps.

Return Gifts

Plaster of Paris Colour-It-Yourself Kits

Pre-cast shapes — unicorns, dinos, stars and more — with paint and a brush, ready for party guests to color on the spot. Sold as sets, sized to your guest count.

Room Décor

Custom Name Wall Hangers

A child's name, hand-strung in thread and beads, in colors you choose. Made to order, sized for a nursery or bedroom wall.

Back-to-School

Name Bag Tags with Clickables

A personalized tag for a backpack or lunchbox, with fun clip-on charms kids can swap and collect.

Peace of Mind

NFC Emergency Bag Tags

A tap-to-view tag for a backpack — no app needed. Carries just a first name and one parent phone number, nothing more.

Privacy, built into every safety tag

Our NFC bag tags are designed to protect, not expose. Each one carries only a first name and a single parent phone number — never a home address, school name, birth date, or ID number. Peace of mind for parents, without the risk.
